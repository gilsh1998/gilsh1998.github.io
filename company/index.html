How It Works

1

Intelligence Gathering

RADAR scans news, sentiment, and SEC filings every 15 minutes. QUANT runs technical analysis — SMA 150 crossovers, RSI, volume patterns. Both report to ORACLE.

2

Decision Making

ORACLE synthesizes intelligence into actionable briefings and sends them to APEX. APEX reviews past lessons for the ticker, evaluates the opportunity, and decides whether to trade.

3

Risk Assessment

Every trade must pass through SHIELD — the risk manager with veto power. SHIELD checks stop-losses, position sizing, cash reserves, portfolio concentration, and scores risk 0–100.

4

Execution & Monitoring

BROKER executes approved trades with full validation (7 checks). PULSE monitors system health 24/7, auto-restarts crashed agents, and MIKE escalates critical issues via Telegram.

ORACLE
Head of Intelligence

Monitors Reuters RSS, SEC EDGAR 8-K filings, and Stocktwits sentiment in real-time. Flags breaking news, sentiment shifts, and unusual social activity. Reports to ORACLE.

QUANT
Technical Analyst
Claude Sonnet

Runs technical analysis via Yahoo Finance — SMA 20/50/150 crossovers, RSI readings, ATR for volatility, and volume confirmation. The numbers behind every trade decision.

Trading Department

— Decisions, risk & execution
APEX
Head of Trading
Claude Opus

The decision-maker. Receives intelligence briefings from ORACLE, reviews past lessons for each ticker, and decides whether to buy or sell. Every order includes a technical reason and must pass SHIELD's approval.

SHIELD
Risk Manager
Claude Opus

Has absolute veto power over every trade. Validates stop-losses (-7%), position sizing ($5K–$10K), cash reserves ($20K minimum), and portfolio concentration. Scores risk 0–100.

BROKER
Trade Executor
Claude Sonnet

Executes SHIELD-approved trades with 7 mandatory validation checks: trading hours, reason quality, trade size, position limits, cash reserve, position count, and SHIELD approval.

Operations Department

MIKE
Head of Operations

The operational backbone. Oversees system health, coordinates inter-agent communication, and escalates critical issues directly to Gil via Telegram. Manages PULSE and NOVA.
