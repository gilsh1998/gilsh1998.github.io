How It Works

Four stages, fully autonomous.

1

Intelligence Gathering

RADAR scans news, sentiment, and SEC filings every 15 min. QUANT runs technical analysis — SMA 150 crossovers, RSI, volume. Both report to ORACLE.

2

Decision Making

ORACLE synthesizes intelligence into briefings and sends them to APEX. APEX reviews past lessons, evaluates the opportunity, and decides whether to trade.

3

Risk Assessment

Every trade passes through SHIELD — the risk manager with veto power. SHIELD checks stops, position sizing, cash reserves, and scores risk 0–100.

4

Execution & Monitoring

BROKER executes approved trades with 7 mandatory checks. PULSE monitors system health 24/7, MIKE escalates critical issues to Gil via Telegram.
